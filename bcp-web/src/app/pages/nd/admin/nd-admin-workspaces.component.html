AI credit price

Sets what a credit is worth and how much we add on top of the provider's real cost. It applies to new AI calls; calls already recorded keep the price they were charged at.

  • $1 = {{ previewCreditsPerDollar | number: '1.0-2' }} credits, so a $5 credit pack is {{ previewCreditsPerDollar * 5 | number: '1.0-0' }} credits.
  • An AI call that costs us $1.00 is charged as ${{ previewBilled | number: '1.2-2' }} ({{ previewBilled / priceUsdPerCredit | number: '1.0-0' }} credits). We keep ${{ previewBilled - 1 | number: '1.2-2' }} ({{ previewMarginPct | number: '1.0-1' }}% of what the client pays).
  • If a provider raises its price, the same call costs more credits, so the margin stays the same. Nothing is charged at a fixed price.

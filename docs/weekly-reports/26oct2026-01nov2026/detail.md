Weekly Update - 26 Oct 2026 to 01 Nov 2026

Rulebook categories

Administrators can now organise their regulations by rulebook area. A new Rulebook categories page under Admin settings holds each business workspace's own category structure, up to three levels deep: a category such as Banking, a sub-category such as Capital Adequacy, and the rulebook itself, such as Regulations Re Capital Adequacy. Categories can be added at any level, renamed, given a short description, and deleted. Deleting asks for confirmation first and says how many categories sit underneath, since those are removed too.

To get started quickly, a workspace without any categories can import the structure of the Central Bank of the UAE Rulebook with one click: All Licensed Financial Institutions, Banking, Insurance and Other Regulated Entities, with their 41 sub-categories and 164 rulebooks. The imported structure belongs to that workspace only and can be edited freely afterwards. Once a workspace has categories, the import option no longer appears.

Each workspace's categories are visible only to that workspace, and only its administrators can change them.

Regulation documents by rulebook

Regulation documents can now be filed under rulebook categories instead of departments. On the Regulation Documents page each document shows its rulebook categories, and an administrator or maker can assign or change them from a searchable list of the whole category tree. A document can sit in more than one category, at any level. The list can be filtered by rulebook; choosing a category also shows everything filed under its sub-categories.

Once the sample categories are imported, the Rulebook categories page also offers to import the rulebook documents themselves, one per rulebook. Each document is added to Regulation Documents under its rulebook and prepared for analysis automatically. Where the Central Bank's site does not allow an automatic download, each rulebook has a Source link to open the official page and an Upload document button to add the file you downloaded, which is then filed and prepared in the same way.

Permanent deletion

Deleted internal and regulation documents can now be removed for good by the platform administrator from the Deleted lists. This is only possible for documents that are no longer used in any analysis, so existing reports keep their sources. Business administrators continue to delete documents in the usual way, which keeps them recoverable.

Departments for internal documents

Departments now work the same way as rulebook categories, up to three levels: a department such as Compliance, a unit such as AML/CFT, and a team such as Sanctions Screening. A workspace without departments can start from a ready-made set of typical bank departments with one click. On the Internal Documents page each document shows its departments, they can be assigned from a searchable, collapsible list, and the list can be filtered by department.

Getting rulebook documents in faster

The Central Bank's site only allows documents to be downloaded from a person's browser. The Rulebook categories page now has a Download PDF link on each rulebook and an Upload downloaded PDFs button that takes many files at once and files each one under the right rulebook automatically, based on its file name. Every level of the structure can also hold its own document.

Regulatory Library and accurate overview numbers

The left menu now has a Regulatory Library section below Documents, with Regulatory Authorities, Regulation Documents and Regulatory Selective Points. Regulatory Authorities starts from the Central Bank of the UAE, the Dubai Financial Services Authority, Abu Dhabi Global Market and the Capital Market Authority, each with its rulebooks; the Central Bank includes its full published rulebook structure. Regulation documents can also be viewed grouped by rulebook in a collapsible tree.

The overview cards now count only real regulation findings. An older type of analysis also recorded internal policy sections that no regulation covers, and these were being added to the gap, action and compliance totals, which made some numbers far too high. Opening the Actions card now takes you straight to the corrective actions list, and its resolved and pending figures open the matching tab.
